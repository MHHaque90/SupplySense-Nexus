SupplySense Nexus

AI-Powered Supply Chain Resilience and Decision Intelligence

Phase 0: Repository Audit and Foundation

Platform Overview

SupplySense Nexus helps supply-chain teams understand demand, inventory, suppliers, procurement, disruptions, and operational trade-offs.

All simulated or synthetic results are clearly labeled as simulated, estimated, or illustrative.

Core Modules

  • Data Foundation - Sales, inventory, suppliers, products, warehouses
  • Demand Intelligence - Forecasting, seasonality, trend analysis
  • Inventory Intelligence - Stock levels, safety stock, stockout risk
  • Supplier and Procurement Intelligence - Scorecards, MOQ, alternatives
  • Supply-Chain Risk Graph - Dependencies, single points of failure
  • Disruption and Scenario Lab - Simulated disruption analysis
  • Decision Replay Lab - Compare decisions under same scenario
  • Optimization Engine - Supplier selection, allocation, replenishment
  • AI Decision Copilot - Natural-language analytical assistant
  • Governance and Evaluation - Audit, model registry, drift monitoring

Architecture

Backend: Python 3.14, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL

Frontend: HTML, CSS, Vanilla JavaScript, Chart.js

Analytics: Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Statsmodels

Optimization: OR-Tools (planned)

Deployment: Docker, Docker Compose

Project Status

Current Phase: Phase 0 - Repository Audit, Product Definition, System Architecture, and Development Foundation
